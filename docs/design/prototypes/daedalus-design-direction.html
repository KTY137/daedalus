One law: three reading distances

Every component has to declare which distance it serves, and it may only serve one. This is the rule the old screen had no way to express, so everything defaulted to the middle and competed.

From three metres

The bezel

One live state and nothing else. Graphite and phosphor. If a second indicator wants to live here, one of them is not urgent.

Sitting down

The document

Findings as prose, on paper, in the serif. Written from your side of the screen — no field names, no JSON, no internal vocabulary.

When you doubt it

The well

The raw measurement, inset into the page like a gauge let into a bench. Mono, tabular, closed by default, one click away, always there.

Two materials, never nested

A dark chassis holding a lit document, adjacent rather than stacked. This is what keeps the page out of the near-black-plus-one-glowing-accent look every agent dashboard wears — there is a large warm surface carrying the content, and the accents are oxidised metals rather than a hue picked for contrast. In the light theme the housing becomes brushed aluminium; the paper stays paper, because paper does not invert.

A serif and a mono. No sans anywhere.

The absence is the decision. Grotesk — Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk — is the uniform of every generated dashboard, and the fastest way to look like one. Daedalus already speaks in two registers, so the type system is those two registers and nothing more: a humanist serif for everything the system says in words, a mono for everything it measured. Labels are set in the serif with letterspacing rather than switching to a third face. Production self-hosts the faces; this page uses the closest system stack.

What got cut on the way here

  • The four-tile metric row. Telemetry / System proof / Provider sample / Promotion as equal boxes is the most recognisable trope in the category. One dial in the rail carries the number that mattered.
  • Twenty-five borders. Nesting ran space → section → evidence panel → table row. Two materials replace all of it; the only remaining border is where a material ends.
  • The permanent status legend. Five pills with counts, above the content, before anything was said. The vocabulary now appears where it applies, in the margin.
  • Shouted labels as decoration. MISSION QUEUE, SELF-IMPROVEMENT LOOP, CONNECTIONS, WATCHER, EVIDENCE — WHAT THIS WAS MEASURED FROM. Kept three, in small caps, quiet.
  • Raw JSON on the glass. {"line":695,"module_path":…} sat in a table cell. It still exists, one click down, formatted.
  • The suggestion chips. “What's running?”, “Build me an agent network.” Nothing says generated product louder.
