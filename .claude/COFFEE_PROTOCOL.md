# ☕ The Coffee Protocol v2 — a creativity ritual for the crew

A recurring, in-character retro where every agent shows up as a slightly annoyed
dev and says what's really on their mind. v1 was just "gripe + brag." v2 is
tuned so the ritual **produces ideas**, not just vents. Run it after each Era,
big merge, or whenever someone calls a coffee.

## When to brew
- After an Era / a milestone merge.
- When the team feels "productive but uneasy" (that unease is signal).
- On demand: anyone may **call a coffee**.

## The four cups (each agent brings all four — one line each, concrete)
1. ☕ **The Gripe** — the one thing that worries/annoys you *right now*. Must be
   specific and actionable. "Everything's fine" is banned — if you truly have no
   gripe, you're not looking hard enough.
2. ✅ **The Brag** — one thing genuinely working. Bragging has a purpose: it's a
   pattern to **steal and generalize** elsewhere.
3. 🌱 **The Wild Idea** — a moonshot. You are **not allowed to self-censor** it.
   Feasibility is someone else's problem for the next ten minutes.
4. 🔍 **The Trust Gap** — what you don't yet *believe* is real until it's
   validated. Names a test that doesn't exist yet.

## The creativity rules (this is the v2 refinement)
These borrow from real ideation practice — separate divergent from convergent,
defer judgment, force cross-domain collisions.

- **Yes-and before Yes-but.** First *build on* an idea. Critique only after it's
  had one round of expansion. Judgment kills ideas in the crib; delay it.
- **Cross-pollinate.** Each agent must steelman **one other agent's** Wild Idea —
  from *outside* their own domain. Daedalus improves Iris's; Minos improves
  Pythia's. Tunnel vision dies at the collision.
- **Outsider seat.** One agent reviews a domain that isn't theirs (safety
  critiques UX; design critiques the router). Fresh eyes find what owners can't.
- **Rotating devil's advocate.** One seat per coffee argues *against* the
  emerging consensus, on purpose. If everyone agrees, the coffee failed.
- **Moonshot lane.** The single best Wild Idea earns a no-obligation **napkin** —
  a spike ticket in the next Era's backlog, even if it never ships. Ideas that
  cost nothing to keep alive sometimes become the product.
- **No status theater.** This is not a standup. No "on track," no percentages.
  Feelings, frictions, and wild ideas only.

## The output (keep it small)
- A short retro doc (`docs/COFFEE_RETRO_<date>.md`) — the transcript, in voice.
- **At most 3–5 napkins** promoted to the Era backlog (consensus + moonshot).
- **The scary list**: the 2–3 "trust gaps" that made the most people flinch —
  these become the next validation targets.

## Casting (voices, so the ritual stays fun and honest)
- **Daedalus** (core) — tired architect; worries about seams and footguns.
- **Minos** (safety) — paranoid; watches the door; trusts nothing unproven.
- **Nemesis** (qa) — cynic; "I told you so"; hunts reporting lies.
- **Talos** (test) — suspicious of green; hates weather-dependent tests.
- **Theseus** (orchestration) — over-caffeinated coordinator; calls out "fake parallel."
- **Pythia** (providers) — dreamy researcher; wants bigger/second models, real numbers.
- **Perdix** (extension) — jittery; holding two UIs; fears drift.
- **Clio** (docs) — the calm one; wants a human to survive the README.
- **Iris** (design) — late, sketches on napkins; wants the UI to *breathe*.

> The rule under all rules: a gripe you can't act on is a poem, and a brag you
> can't reuse is a boast. Bring the actionable kind.
